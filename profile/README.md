# ChatDok

**Physician-led clinical AI and digital therapeutics for cardiovascular and chronic care.**

ChatDok GmbH builds AI-enabled digital health tools that support patients between clinical encounters and give care teams a clearer picture of how patients are doing. Our AI does not diagnose or prescribe. It supports patients, clinicians and health systems.

🌐 [chatdok.com](https://chatdok.com) · ✉️ [hello@chatdok.com](mailto:hello@chatdok.com)

---

### What we build

| Product | For | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatDok App** | Patients | A multilingual health companion for everyday questions and chronic-condition support |
| **ChatDok-Heart** | Patients and cardiologists | AI-assisted cardiovascular care, with a clinical cockpit for remote monitoring and care plans |
| **ChatDok-Navigator** | Hospitals, health systems and insurers | AI support for patients after hospital discharge |

### Open resources

Our product code is private. These repositories hold research documentation, reusable building blocks and evaluation tools that we share with the research and health-tech community.

| Repository | What's inside |
|---|---|
| [cardiovascular-health-data-research](https://github.com/chatdok-health/cardiovascular-health-data-research) | The research value of ChatDok's longitudinal conversational data, and how we approach curation and access. Documentation only, no patient data |
| [cardio-symptom-lexicon](https://github.com/chatdok-health/cardio-symptom-lexicon) | English and German patient-language phrases for cardiovascular symptoms, with red-flag tags and a small matcher |
| [cardio-safety-eval](https://github.com/chatdok-health/cardio-safety-eval) | Synthetic test cases and a scorer that check whether a health chatbot escalates cardiovascular red flags |
| [health-text-redact](https://github.com/chatdok-health/health-text-redact) | Removes direct identifiers from English and German health conversations before analysis |
| [heart-failure-self-monitoring-fhir](https://github.com/chatdok-health/heart-failure-self-monitoring-fhir) | HL7 FHIR R4 Questionnaire and example resources for daily heart-failure self-monitoring |
