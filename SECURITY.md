# Security policy

ChatDok handles health information, so we take security reports seriously and are grateful to anyone who reports a problem responsibly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hello@chatdok.com** with the subject line `Security report`. Please include:

- what you found and where (product, URL, app version or repository);
- the steps needed to reproduce it;
- the impact you think it has.

Please **do not** open a public GitHub issue for security problems.

## What to expect

- We confirm receipt within 5 working days.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix the issue.
- We credit you once the issue is fixed, if you would like that.

## Please do not

- access, change or delete data that does not belong to you, including any patient data;
- run denial-of-service tests, spam or social-engineering attempts against our staff or users;
- share details of the issue publicly before we have fixed it.

## Scope

This policy covers ChatDok's apps, websites and APIs, and the repositories in this GitHub organisation.
